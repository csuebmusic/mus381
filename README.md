# MUS 381 · Essentials of Music Technology

Course materials, teaching tools, and instructor notes for MUS 381 at CSU East Bay: readings, handouts, interactive tools, listening assignments, projects, exams, and session-by-session teaching notes, organized by module. The student-facing pages are served through GitHub Pages at [`csuebmusic.github.io/mus381`](https://csuebmusic.github.io/mus381/).

Students start with the [syllabus](https://csuebmusic.github.io/mus381/syllabus.html) for the schedule, grading, policies, and what to bring. This README is the teaching-side map.

## the course

MUS 381 is a 3-unit introduction to music technology for students with little or no prior experience: digital audio, editing, mixing, recording, sampling, and DAW work, taught hands-on and project by project. Students listen, analyze, and make their own work in response throughout.

The semester runs in four modules. Modules 2, 3, and 4 each end in a deliverable. The class meets Mondays and Wednesdays, 3:00 to 4:40 PM, in MB2525. The weekly pattern of lectures and labs, the dated calendar, grading weights, and course policies are in the [syllabus](https://csuebmusic.github.io/mus381/syllabus.html).

### Module 4: the DAW · Wks 11–14

Three labs in Ableton Live 11 (audio editing and warping, sampling with Simpler and Drum Rack from a MIDI keyboard, mixing), working from each student's own Module 3 library. An optional handout shows the same concepts in Adobe Audition. The deliverable is the final project, a piece built in Live in one draft and a final version. The cumulative final exam is a separate assessment during finals week.

## conventions

Build conventions (naming, document chrome, dates, the visual system, audience and level, sources, and the repo workflow) are in [`under-the-hood/meta/build-conventions.md`](under-the-hood/meta/build-conventions.md), with page templates beside it and per-asset specs in [`assets/asset-recipes.md`](assets/asset-recipes.md). Anyone drafting new student-facing material reads them first. Teaching from the finished pages doesn't require them.

Student-facing pages are HTML sharing `assets/style.css`. Instructor notes, specs, and policies are Markdown.

The file workflow students follow is on the [Session Routines card](https://csuebmusic.github.io/mus381/module-01-fluency/lessons/02-handout-session-routines.html).

## for the instructor

To prep a week, open the module's `README.md`: it has the session-by-session plan (what to say, where students get stuck, how to use each tool, what to assess and how).

Faculty reference texts for the materials (not assigned to students): Hosken, *An Introduction to Music Technology* (primary); Huber, *Modern Recording Techniques*; Holmes, ed., *The Routledge Guide to Music Technology*; Holmes, *Technology, Music, and Culture*; *The Cambridge Companion to Electronic Music*.
